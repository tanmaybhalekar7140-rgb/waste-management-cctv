From camera to clean-up

Each step runs in seconds, so waste is handled while it is still a small problem.

  1. CaptureCameras already on site record the bins and the area around them.
  2. DetectThe system finds plastic, mixed waste and bins that are full.
  3. AlertStaff get the location, the camera and a snapshot.
  4. CollectA cleaner goes to the spot and clears it.
  5. ReportEvery alert is logged, so busy hours and hotspots become clear.

Built to look at waste, not people.

Cameras are used only to find waste. Footage is shared with site authorities with permission, kept for a limited time, and faces are never the point of an alert. Larger systems should add face blurring before any wider rollout.
